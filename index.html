1. Multi-Head GAT Attention Mechanism

Standard GCN averages all neighbors equally, causing over-smoothing. GAT learns parameterized edge attention coefficients $\alpha_{ij}$ to focus on illicit fund dispersion paths:

$$\alpha_{ij} = \frac{\exp(\mathbf{a}^T [\mathbf{W}\mathbf{h}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{W}\mathbf{h}_j])}{\sum_{k \in \mathcal{N}_i} \exp(\mathbf{a}^T [\mathbf{W}\mathbf{h}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{W}\mathbf{h}_k])}$$
Filters decoy noise & isolates merchant abuse clusters

2. Mathematical Cost Model & Imbalance

With 0.13% fraud rate (770:1 imbalance), standard 0.50 threshold collapses. The cost-optimal threshold minimizes financial loss:

$$T^* = \arg\min_T \left[ \text{FP}(T) \cdot C_{\text{FP}} + \text{FN}(T) \cdot C_{\text{FN}} \right] \quad (C_{\text{FP}}=₹350, \; C_{\text{FN}}=₹42,000)$$
Cost-Optimal $T^* = 0.42$ delivers ₹48,200+ savings per 10k batch
