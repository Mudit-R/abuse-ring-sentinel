Calibration & Cost-Aware Decision Math

Mathematical threshold derivation under INR 350 FP review cost vs INR 42,000 FN fraud loss:

T*Bayes = CFP / (CFP + CFN) = 350 / (350 + 42,000) ≈ 0.008264 (0.83%)
Pure single-transaction Bayes boundary is 0.83%. However, flagging all transactions >0.83% produces thousands of alerts exceeding daily investigation capacity. Our cascade operates at T* = 0.42 to minimize total batch loss under realistic shift quotas.
